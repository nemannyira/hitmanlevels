# Anathema Reworked (C1-1 turned into a new mission)

The base level's own story is off; six objectives of different kinds fill the
laptop: take the Don's ledger (an item), get into a bodyguard's suit (a
disguise), hurt no innocent (a rule), be out in eight minutes (a time
limit), eliminate the new consigliere and check the garage (editor goals).
Then back up the hill. Hitman starts as the postman (Level tab > *Hitman
starts in*).

Play: copy this folder into the game's `Mods` folder, then main menu > Level
Editor > Open a level > *Play*.

Played through 2026-10-13 (h2/docs/playtest_log.md): completed. The ledger
lies on the road by the garage: walk onto it and press E (the game offers
items from about a metre).
