Editor & tools

The level editor runs inside Hitman 2: Silent Assassin. One zip holds it, the installer of shared levels and five example missions.

What is in it

  • ■
    dinput8.dll and H2Fix.iniThe editor, and fixes for today's PCs (frame limit, borderless window, widescreen field of view, larger menus). Next to hitman2.exe.
  • ■
    levelpack.exeInstalls the .h2lvl levels you download; checks each author's signature first.
  • ■
    Five example missionsThe Accountant, Dead Drop, Estate Run, The Informant, Arena: folders to copy into the game's Mods.
  • ■
    README, licensesInstalling step by step; MIT, and the libraries' own licenses.

How to install it and play a level: Get started.

Not code-signed yet

Windows SmartScreen or an antivirus may warn about the files until they are signed. Check that your download is the published one: its SHA-256 must match the one here.

Get-FileHash hitmanlevels-h2-...zip

The editor itself never connects to the internet; levelpack only when you ask it to list or install levels from this site.
