Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Hitman Levels (hitmanlevels.com) keeps as little about you as it can: no passwords, no e-mail addresses, no tracking, no advertising. This notice says what it keeps, why, for how long, who else handles it and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The controller is Pintér Tamás, a private person in Hungary who runs the site. Contact: privacy@hitmanlevels.com.

Playing levels: nothing about you

Looking at the site and downloading levels needs no account. The site keeps a count of each level's downloads, with nothing about who downloaded it. The site sets no cookie for you then.

When you sign in, the service you choose (Discord, GitHub or Google) tells the site only your account's number and name. The site asks for nothing more: not your e-mail address, contacts or anything else.

Cookies

  • __Host-oauth: for ten minutes while you sign in, to check that the answer from Discord, GitHub or Google belongs to the sign-in you started.
  • __Host-session: keeps you signed in (30 days).

Both are needed for signing in and are set only when you sign in. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else handles the data

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site, its database and its files (as processor). The database is kept in Cloudflare's eastern European region; Cloudflare's network serves the site worldwide and, as every web host, sees the IP address of each request to protect the site. Cloudflare transfers data to the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU's standard contractual clauses. The site's own database stores no IP addresses.
  • Discord, GitHub (Microsoft) or Google, whichever you sign in with, handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies.

Nothing is sold or given to anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

What stays public

A level you upload is public: its title, texts, picture, author name and author key can be seen and downloaded by anyone, and players keep the copies they downloaded. If a level is removed, the site stops offering it, but it cannot take back copies already on players' computers.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what the site keeps about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a machine-readable form. Write to privacy@hitmanlevels.com from the account's sign-in service or with a way to show the account is yours; you get an answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Hungarian supervisory authority: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11, naih.hu, or to the authority of the EU country where you live.

Age

Accounts are for people of 16 or older. The Hitman games themselves are rated for adults.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new one is posted here with its date. A change in what is kept or why is announced on the site before it applies.
