# The Accountant: Escalation (C1-1)

[The Accountant](../TheAccountant) as an escalation: three stages, each the
one before and more.

1. **The Books**: eliminate the accountant on the road by the garage, then
   get back to the hill where you start.
2. **Quietly**: a bodyguard walks with him, and nobody may see you (seen: the
   stage fails).
3. **On the Clock**: five minutes; no innocents killed (one killed is noted:
   the stage done does not count as clean).

Play: copy this folder into the game's `Mods` folder, then main menu > Level
Editor > Open a level: the launcher shows the stage to play (the next one;
the stages done can be played again).

Made with the editor: The Accountant's level, *Make it an escalation* and
three stages with their texts and rules (Mission tab > *Stages*). `main.lua`
is The Accountant's and makes the bodyguard from stage 2 on (`h2.stage()`).
