# Arena (C1-1 with no buildings, on the stone yard of C3-3)

An open yard in the sky. Three armed bodyguards wait on the far side:
eliminate all three, then leave by the far corner.

Play: copy this folder into the game's `Mods` folder, then main menu > Level
Editor > Open a level > *Play*. (The yard comes from C3-3: the map loads with
it.)

Made with the editor only (but a few lines of story): main menu > Level
Editor > New level with *Empty map*, *No buildings either* and the C3-3 stone
yard; three guards (NPCs tab: BodyguardMafia_01 with BaseGuard); a point in
the far corner; *Make it a target* three times and *Make it the exit*
(Inspector > Mission); the objectives' texts (Mission tab).

Played through 2026-10-12 (docs/playtest_log.md): completed (Silent
Assassin rating). Pillars copied from the map for cover stayed unseen on the
blank map (open, P17): they were left out.
