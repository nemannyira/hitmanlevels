Badges

What your plays of the players' levels earn. They count the plays levelpack sends signed in to your account with their ticket (the site checks their time), from when the badges came; a play with testing aids or the editor's developer settings is never sent. Badges are public, on your player page and on the levels' pages (the share of a level's players who have each).

On a level

Each by one play of the level (an escalation's: of one stage).

Mastery of a level: its badges you have, as one to five marks (1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 of them; an escalation's stages counted together). A badge a level's page shows grey cannot be earned there now: its hover says why (Under Par before the level has 10 finished runs, First to Finish once someone has it, Precision on a level with no target of its own to kill). Suit Only, Precision and No Bodies Found come from plays of the editor's newer versions, which tell the site what Hitman wore, killed and left behind.
