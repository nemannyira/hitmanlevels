Get started

Four steps from a fresh install of Hitman 2: Silent Assassin to playing a level from this site, and to making your own.

  1. Put the editor next to the game

    The editor is one file the game loads by itself, with its settings beside it. Download it and copy both files into the game's folder, next to hitman2.exe:

    C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Hitman 2 Silent Assassin\

    Download the editor  dinput8.dll and H2Fix.ini

    It changes none of the game's own files. To remove it, delete the two files.

  2. Get a level

    Pick a level on the Levels page and download it: a .h2lvl file. Open it with levelpack.exe (it comes with the editor), or from a command line:

    levelpack install Snow_Ambush_v1.h2lvl

    It shows the level, its author and its license before it installs. The first level of an author asks whether you trust that author; later ones install without asking.

    A level changed by anyone but its author is refused: every level is signed. A downloaded level's scripts run with less memory and time than your own, and without the console.

  3. Play it

    Start the game. In the main menu choose Level Editor, then Open a level, pick it and press Play. Its map loads with the level's people, objectives and briefing on the laptop.

    F8 opens the editor on the level while you play; F1 lists the keys.

  4. Make your own

    In the main menu choose Level Editor, then New level: pick a map and Simple. A guide takes you through the mission, the people, the items and trying it.

    Its last step, Save & share, writes a package of your level, signed with your own author key. Upload it here.
